Frequently asked questions

Good questions. Clear answers.

Can every negative Google review be removed?

No. A review is not eligible for removal simply because it is negative or a business disagrees with it. The platform reviews reports against its policies and makes the final decision.

Can you guarantee a review will be removed?

No reputable service can guarantee a platform’s decision. We can help assess the situation and explain the available reporting options, but the final decision belongs to the platform.

Do you need my Google password?

Never send your password through this website or a message. Start by sharing the public business profile link and the review details. If a service step ever requires account access, use the platform’s official, secure permission process.

How long does the process take?

Timing varies by case and by the platform’s review process. We’ll explain what to expect, but we cannot promise a specific timeline.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the case and the agreed scope. Contact us for an assessment and written pricing before any work begins.

Do you work with businesses outside Pakistan?

Contact us with your business location and needs. We’ll confirm whether we can support your request.
